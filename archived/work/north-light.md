North Light came to us with a roadmap nobody believed in. Seven leaders, seven
versions of the product in their heads, and a team caught in the middle building all
of them at once.

## What we did

- Ran a structured alignment sprint to surface the real disagreements, not the polite ones.
- Prototyped three sharply different directions so the choice became concrete.
- Wrote the one-page brief everyone could finally point to and say "that one."

Strategy work rarely makes a portfolio because it has no screenshots. But getting
seven people to agree on a single sentence was the hardest design problem here.
