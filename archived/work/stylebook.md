Stylebook lets independent salons run their whole business from one place - bookings,
inventory, payouts. The product worked, but the team's own staff dreaded opening it.

## What we did

- Ran a week of contextual interviews and turned the complaints into a ranked backlog.
- Designed a new information architecture around the salon's day, not the database's tables.
- Shipped a design system so the in-house team could keep moving without us.

By the end, the people who said "I hate this system" were the ones asking if they
could show it off to other salons.
