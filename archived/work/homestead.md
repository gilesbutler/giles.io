Buying a first home means facing a wall of numbers you've never seen before - escrow,
PMI, points, closing costs. Homestead wanted to make that wall feel like a staircase.

## What we did

- Interviewed 22 first-time buyers mid-purchase to find exactly where confidence broke.
- Designed plain-language explainers that sit inline, right where the jargon appears.
- Built an affordability model people could actually argue with their partner about.

The metric we cared about wasn't time-on-page. It was whether someone could explain
their own mortgage back to us. Most could.
