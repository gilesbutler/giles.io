Northwind's charting tool had grown by accretion - every feature request bolted on
another modal, another required field, another click between a clinician and the
patient in front of them. We started by sitting in on real shifts and counting the
taps it took to close a single encounter.

## What we did

- Mapped the seven most common workflows and cut the median click count by 41%.
- Rebuilt the encounter screen around a single, glanceable timeline.
- Designed a keyboard-first interaction model for the power users who live in the app all day.

The result wasn't a flashy redesign - it was a quiet one. The kind nobody notices,
because the software finally gets out of the way.
